Important Information for Speaker Cleaner Users

App Privacy Developer Privacy Terms Disclaimer

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Important: Speaker Cleaner is a software utility, not a hardware repair service.

1. Purpose

The App plays audio frequencies and may use device vibration to attempt to move small amounts of water or loose debris from speaker openings.

2. No Repair Guarantee

The App cannot guarantee that water or debris will be removed or that speaker performance will improve. Hardware damage and corrosion require appropriate technical assessment.

3. Water Exposure

Follow the device manufacturer's water exposure instructions. Do not charge a wet device or assume the App makes the device safe to use.

4. Volume and Hearing Safety

Start at moderate volume. Avoid prolonged loud playback. Stop immediately if you experience discomfort, distortion, or unusual device behavior.

Avoid wearing headphones during speaker-cleaning sound playback.

5. Device Compatibility

Results vary depending on speaker design, Android version, volume output, and device condition. Not every feature behaves identically on all devices.

6. Professional Assistance

If a device has persistent audio issues, liquid damage, corrosion, or other physical problems, contact the manufacturer or a qualified repair technician.

7. Responsibility

Users should exercise reasonable care and follow device safety guidance. The App does not replace professional diagnostics.
