1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how information is processed when you use the Speaker Cleaner application ("App"), developed and published by an independent developer ("we", "us", or "our").

2. App Functionality

Speaker Cleaner offers audio-based speaker cleaning, water-ejection sounds, dust-ejection sounds, manual frequency controls, speaker tests, and optional vibration features.

These features play or generate audio locally. They do not require a user account.
